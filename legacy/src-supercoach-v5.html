Rules & Scoring
🏇 How It Works

Spring Racing Carnival SuperStable is a fantasy game based on the real prizemoney earned during Victoria's Spring Racing Carnival — from Memsie Stakes Day at Caulfield (29 August) through to C.F. Orr Stakes Day at Caulfield (14 November). You build a stable of horses within a fixed salary cap by winning them in a live auction draft against rival managers. Horses score 100% of their prizemoney, so every dollar your stable earns on track is a dollar on your scoreboard.

👥 Stable Structure
🐎
10 Horses
Your whole stable, won in the live auction draft. Every horse scores based on 100% of prizemoney earned across the carnival — there's no bench, so every pick counts every week. Horses can be transferred in and out during the season — see Stable Transfer rules below.
💰 Prizemoney Splits

⭐ The Captain

One horse in your stable is designated your Captain each week. Your Captain's prizemoney is multiplied by 2×, making it the most important selection decision you make each week.

Example: If your Captain earns $3M in prizemoney in a given race, their score is doubled to $6M.

You can change your Captain before each week's Friday 5pm AEDT deadline. In My Stable, drag any horse into the gold Captain slot, or drag your current Captain out to replace them. You can also designate a Vice Captain — if your Captain is scratched, your Vice Captain steps up automatically.

💵 Salary Cap
💰
Salary Cap: $100
This is the cap every manager drafts with — spend it winning bids on your 10 horses in the live auction draft
📊
Horse Pricing
Horses have no preset price. Every horse opens the auction at $1 and the market decides what it's worth — bid on what you rate, not a catalogue number
🚫
No Overspending
You cannot bid more than your remaining cap during the draft, once at least $1 is set aside for every remaining empty slot
⏰ Selection Deadline

🔄 Stable Transfers — Horses Only
🐎
One Horse Transfer Per Week
Once the carnival is underway, you may swap out one horse from your stable each week. Transfers open after each race day and close at 5:00 PM AEDT on the Friday before that week's feature race day.
💰
Cap Allocation
Horses have no preset price, so the incoming horse simply inherits the cap you had allocated to the outgoing horse — your total cap spend doesn't change on a transfer.
⭐
Captain Change is Free
Changing your Captain does not count as your weekly transfer. You can swap your Captain and still make a horse transfer in the same week — they are independent actions, both subject to the Friday 5pm deadline.
⏩
Unused Transfers Don't Roll Over
If you don't use your weekly horse transfer, it lapses — you cannot bank transfers and use two in a later week.
📋
Transfer History
All transfers are logged in the Stable Transfer tab so you can track every change made to your stable across the season.
Example: You transfer out a horse you paid $14 for at auction. The horse you bring in inherits that $14 allocation — your overall cap spend stays the same.

🐎 Horse Pool Import
Upload a spreadsheet of horse names to add to the draftable pool — a horse_name (or just "horse") column is all that's required. Optional columns for trainer, key_races, tags (comma list, e.g. gr1,cup,mare) and notes will be used if present. This never removes or overwrites existing horses — only adds names it hasn't seen before.
📋 Weekly Acceptances Import
Upload the acceptances export (date, venue, race number, horse name). This replaces "this week's" racing list and auto-adds any horse name not already in the pool. Horses have no preset price — every horse opens the auction at $1 and the market sets the value.
🏆 Cumulative Prizemoney Import
Same layout as acceptances, plus a prizemoney column (name it anything containing "prize", e.g. prizemoney_won). This should be each horse's running season-to-date total, not just fresh winnings — importing a later export replaces the stored figure, so it's always safe to re-import the latest full cumulative file.
⚠️ Reset Imported Data
Clears every horse, this-week race, and earnings total added via import from this browser — the original built-in horse list is untouched. Useful for testing.
